Context Session micro-PoC v9

Only functional change from v8:
- deterministicPointerClick() now rounds the AX-derived center coordinates
  before passing them to `agent-ctrl mouse`.
- This fixes half-pixel values such as 232.5, which the CLI rejects.
- Coordinates remain fully deterministic and are never produced by the LLM.

Context-session, planner, cache experiment, executors and recovery semantics
are otherwise unchanged.
